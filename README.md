# ResolveX — AI-Powered Customer Resolution Engine

> **Understand. Resolve. Predict. Prevent.**

ResolveX is an AI-powered customer support copilot built for telecom and broadband support teams.

It helps support agents understand customer issues faster by bringing together customer details, previous conversations, account information, support tickets, and trusted knowledge base articles in one place.

Based on the situation, ResolveX can:

- Suggest a grounded solution
- Ask for missing information
- Escalate complex issues to a specialist
- Prepare a clear handoff with the full customer context
- Help agents respond faster while keeping a human in control

## 💡 The Problem

Customer support agents often have to switch between different systems to understand a customer's problem.

They may need to check:

- Customer account details
- Billing information
- Previous support tickets
- Conversation history
- Network or service information
- Support documentation

This can make resolution slow and can also lead to customers repeating the same information multiple times.

For complex technical issues, poor handoffs can make the problem even worse because specialists may not receive enough context.

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

ResolveX brings the important information together and helps the support agent decide what should happen next.

The AI analyzes the customer's message, checks the available customer context, finds relevant support knowledge, and recommends one of three actions:

### 1. RESOLUTION READY

When enough information is available, ResolveX prepares a response based on trusted support knowledge.

The agent can review and edit the response before sending it.

### 2. NEEDS INFORMATION

If something important is missing, ResolveX identifies what information is needed and suggests a clear question for the customer.

This avoids guessing and helps move the conversation forward.

### 3. ESCALATION REQUIRED

For complex, repeated, or unresolved issues, ResolveX prepares an escalation summary for a specialist.

The specialist receives the relevant customer information, previous troubleshooting steps, and issue context without making the customer repeat everything.
